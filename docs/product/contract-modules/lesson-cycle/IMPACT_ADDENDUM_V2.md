## Contract delta

- Packet/public entry point and storage ownership remain unchanged.
- Policy identity changes to `lesson-cycle-v2`; V1 checkpoints are not reusable.
- A provisional blind review is followed by a fresh counterexample audit. The
  auditor tries to falsify the key, find another valid option, preserve actor /
  action / permission versus obligation, and prove only actually tested targets.
- Admitted questions retain that audit and exact target-specific question/source
  evidence. Resume revalidates both reviews, not only the key.
- A deterministic conservative guard rejects overlapping percentage predicates,
  even when both model passes say they are exclusive. This guard handles numeric
  literals and common RU round-number words; it is NOT a general logic prover or
  coverage of every numeric unit. Conservative rejection is allowed.
- Normally three logical calls per lesson; at most six including one repair.
  Failed structural/evidence checks can skip the counterexample call. Never
  repeatedly repair until a green verdict appears.
- Operational targets must be atomic. The corrected pilot oracle and additional
  governing source context are versioned separately; unchanged lesson text and
  the frozen old baseline may be reused with explicit lineage, not called a new
  paired replicate. Manual curation is not an automatic whole-course solution.
- `ready_for_methodologist` remains a review candidate, NOT automatic approval.
  Independent original-based manual review is required for experiment acceptance.

No shared-worker/default selection, migration, production or provider-route change.
All new rows remain synthetic QA drafts. Separate deployment/integration, real
process restart, UI and publication-safety gates remain before any default rollout.
