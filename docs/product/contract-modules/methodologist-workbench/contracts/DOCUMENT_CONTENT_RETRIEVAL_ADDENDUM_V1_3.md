## Narrow answer-context policy

Persisted tasks, exchanges and their public representation remain unchanged.
For each new content answer, omit from model recall only earlier turns whose
question equals the current question after whitespace normalization/case folding.
Re-answer against newly retrieved evidence instead of feeding that question's own
potentially wrong answer back as context. Retain other bounded turns, including
their answers, for deictic and short follow-ups. Do not clear the user's archive,
rewrite old answers, change request replay or discard unrelated context.

Remove the contradictory generic instruction that any evidence conflict implies
conflict status. Require disagreement on a fact/dimension the current question
actually asks about, for the same entity/action/conditions/trigger. Who performs
an action is not its duration; a period's start event is not its length. A generic
contrastive example reinforces that signer/start-trigger agreement is supported
even when an unasked duration differs; an explicit later duration question must
then surface that conflict. No example supplies facts or numbers for the answer.
