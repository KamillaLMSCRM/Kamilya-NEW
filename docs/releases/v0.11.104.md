# Release Notes - 0.11.104

**Product version:** 0.11.104
**Git tag:** `v0.11.104`

New course jobs use V11. A lesson retains both its planned topic and authored title before review, so an author cannot silently discard the contextual scope needed by a standalone question. Intentional title correction remains possible. A structurally valid plan that omitted source slices can receive one bounded model allocation without replacing its existing units.

The final editorial task compares the source with separately presented lesson bodies and complete question cards, including authored keys and explanations as untrusted claims. Addressed corrections may alter only material-target lesson bodies or questions; other text, titles, source navigation and original image occurrences remain protected. Unsupported findings remain visible. Genuine source uncertainty and unsolicited editorial suggestions retain their separate authority boundaries.

Malformed evidence quotations no longer require another substantive course edit. When the claim's actual target and other evidence checks are valid, a bounded metadata task selects an already cited owned source passage or leaves the finding unresolved. Original quotes, claims, model selections and hashes are retained; later content edits revalidate the claim. Whole-owned-field typography can accommodate a terminal full stop in final evidence without changing words, numbers, internal punctuation or the proof of a changed field. These checks establish custody, not semantic approval.
