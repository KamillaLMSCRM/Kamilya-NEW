## Product change

Tenant administrators can choose a closed set of printable fields and configure
separate texts for course completion and individual-test results. Preview and
download use the real PDF renderer. Required employee/course/outcome fields
cannot be removed. New result events snapshot the selected template.

Students can download a configured separate-test result immediately after the
attempt and again from its history after reload, before completing the course.
Failed attempts are labelled honestly. A later retry or template change does not
replace a prior result's template or attempt binding. Legacy attempts without a
configured snapshot are not retroactively issued a new form.

Replay idempotency, assignment-token scope classification and misleading local
attempt ordinals are corrected. Accepted quiz deferral days persist on create
and update, including zero; omitted/null updates retain the stored value and
historical-attempt edit guards remain in force. Inputs are bounded0..3650days.
The enrolled-test availability projection also matches submission for zero-day
deadlines, preserving the existing immediate-expiry rule after lesson completion.
