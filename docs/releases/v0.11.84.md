# Release Notes — 0.11.84

**Product version:** 0.11.84
**Git tag when released:** `v0.11.84`

New jobs use focused editorial policy v36. The author receives the approved source and logical groups. Separate source and lesson readers supply literal action/object and noun-attachment hypotheses. The action editor retains whole-course context; the noun editor receives complete owned lessons and the protected course outline. Their proposals preserve questions, titles, order and media. These navigation hypotheses are not source authority, coverage proof or automatic approval.

A question-only editor checks keys and explanations separately from lesson prose. Key-blind checks judge every answer. Current observations return to bounded, owned model repair; unchanged review units reuse their exact observations. Explicit UNVERIFIED question decisions remain actionable even when another review reports no issue. Source, course and question fingerprints prevent stale remarks from acquiring authority over a new revision.

The resulting course remains a draft with explicit editorial review pending. Missing narrative provenance is recorded as course review work, while real conversion, source ambiguity, unreadable-value and cross-document provenance holds stay protected. The existing methodologist approval binds the exact revision; this release does not equate a graph or a model's VALID label with semantic acceptance. Existing v35 and earlier jobs retain their own requests and recovery identities.

Migration 0187 extends the existing runtime-settings catalog and constraints for editorial revision and output budgets, preserving overrides/history. Luna and DeepSeek continue through their existing approved routes with reasoning enabled. There is no provider-plan, resource, permission, voice or reserve configuration change. Unknown post-dispatch outcomes remain unknown and are not silently repeated.

Three variants share one PDF; the cash-course input contained prior human corrections. Cached readings and a failed timeout are disclosed in the experiment receipts. This is a saved-content result, not three fresh native courses or live media proof.

Fresh native DEV runs on PDF, cash instruction and standards26, cost/quality readback, final DeepSeek, protected release and live synthetic acceptance are separate gates recorded in `docs/CODEX_HANDOFF.md`, `docs/testing/TEST_RUN_LEDGER.md` and `docs/PRODUCTION_READINESS.md`.
