# Release Notes — 0.11.92

**Candidate, not production acceptance.** No migration or provider-plan change.

Restore independent editorial behavior when generating from an approved source.
New jobs use separate source-to-lesson review, key-blind option judgments and
explanation review before the addressed editor. The independent option reader
receives only the learner-visible question/title and each candidate answer;
author keys, explanations, lesson prose and author references cannot prime it.
Existing jobs retain their recorded policies and checkpoints.

The server merges edits only into the reviewed domains and targets. Preserve
unaffected content, question IDs/order/keys and original source images. Retain
unresolved reviewer questions and observations as revision-bound editorial
issues, rather than silently closing them or calling them source ambiguity.
Approved interpretation reasons remain available; replaced historical wording
stays in the audit rather than competing with the current approved source.

The new path has five bounded roles, not repeated repair cascades. Review/quotes
and source-reference validation do not confer human approval or publication.
The source worksheet and unsuccessful example-form guide were rejected after
saved negative controls; they are not part of this candidate.

Evidence and limitations: `docs/testing/2026-11-11-engine-restoration.md`,
`GENERATION_ENGINE_CHANGE_HISTORY.md` entries L92C–F. Saved standards and PDF
controls passed independent local content review. The cash example-role error
was repaired by the model from a supplied QA observation; blind discovery of
that error was not reliable. Three fresh full courses in exact DEV, DeepSeek
acceptance and protected production/live checks remain separate release gates.

Published customer courses, provider routing/plans, frontend functionality and
source approval records are outside this patch.
