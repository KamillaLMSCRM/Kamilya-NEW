# Release Notes — 0.11.110

**Product version:** 0.11.110
**Git tag:** `v0.11.110`

Frontend-only correction to the methodologist video guide. Selecting a step seeks to its actual encoded scene boundary and shows the correct frame while paused. Native playback, captions, narration and accepted MP4 files are preserved. Step timestamps are derived offline from the accepted encoded media rather than cumulative audio durations; no model or TTS service is needed in the browser.
