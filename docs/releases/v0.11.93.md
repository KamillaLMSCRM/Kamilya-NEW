# Release Notes — 0.11.93

**Candidate, not production acceptance.** No migration, plan or resource change.

New jobs use three source-bound stages: a meaningful-unit plan, connected lesson
and question authoring, and ordinary editorial correction. The plan selects
literal primary and supporting blocks; its titles never replace the source.
The map is proposed navigation: related topics may be combined, and the editor
may add needed lessons or supporting references from the approved source. Topic
count and initial support edges are not semantic acceptance gates. Shared source text is supplied once
per batch rather than once for every small unit or separate critic.

Keep table parents and authenticated reference targets with the applicable unit.
The compact assignment vector accounts for all source blocks without a schema
node per block. Original source, approved working revision and decision history
remain intact. Replaced historical wording does not compete with the current
source. Redundant owned support is normalized losslessly; no semantic norms are
inferred by that normalization.

The ordinary editor reads the source mapping, lessons, question keys and
explanations and corrects substantive problems while preserving good content,
identities and original illustrations. Only unresolved incompatible source
meanings become source questions. Proposed used-source provenance is checked
against the current owned source and kept distinct from input/dependency scope.
The full literal source is serialized before the candidate text. Original asset
identities remain while misplaced illustrations may be relocated to the right lesson.
Human review and publication requirements are unchanged.

All stages use the existing owned pending journal, exact request and source
binding, complete-response custody and cold replay. Existing jobs retain their
recorded V1/V2 policy. The failed global worksheet and additional critic variants
are not selected by the new path.

Local technical proof includes the real managed-client/producer seam with a
synthetic provider, interrupted author-to-editor continuation, media and forged
request negatives, and a 1,000-source planner schema. Source-only planning and
the accepted literal-unit author/editor experiment do not establish full-course
quality. Exact DEV, three complete independent course reviews, final provider
acceptance and protected production/live verification remain release gates.

Evidence: `docs/testing/GENERATION_ENGINE_CHANGE_HISTORY.md`, CHG-L93C/L93D;
`docs/testing/2026-11-11-engine-restoration.md`. Customer courses, provider plans,
permissions, frontend design and source approval data are outside this patch.

Original document body and generated visual OCR/description remain separate typed
segments. Concatenation retains the exact approved working text; origin labels
do not establish normative authority. The retained cash editorial control passed
independent local content review (10 lessons, 30 questions, ten original asset
markers), with no claim of fresh DEV or production acceptance.
